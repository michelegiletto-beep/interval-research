Reference protocol

The factorial benchmark varies discretization and marginal tail strength. Each seed has separate training, calibration and test scenes.

Status
Loading installed benchmark

Intervals are exploratory bootstrap intervals across independent seed groups. They do not establish a quantum advantage.

What the experiment tests

  1. Mean in rank space versus mean in original intensity.
  2. Classical smoothing, a neural conditional mean and a constrained quantum copula.
  3. Exact probabilities, finite measurements and an explicit readout model.
  4. Performance with thresholds fixed before the test.

Completed local procedures

Research boundary

The included generator is a lognormal observation model. It is not a radar propagation model, a clinical dataset or a physical quantum processor. The reference implementation studies estimators at small circuit sizes. Real-domain conclusions require independent datasets and annotations.
